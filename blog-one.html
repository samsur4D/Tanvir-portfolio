Study of Morphological and Antimicrobial Analysis of Green Synthesized Cu-doped Co-Zn Oxide Nanoparticles using Mikania Micrantha (Leaf) Extract

Abstract

In the field of nanotechnology, particularly in the application of therapeutic, drug transporters, and drug delivery, eco-friendly nanoparticle production techniques have become increasingly popular. In this study, we used the green route for synthesizing Cu-doped Co-ZnO nanoparticles using Mikania micrantha (leaf) extract and tested against gram-positive S. aureus and gram-negative E. coli bacteria. The synthesized material exhibits intriguing morphological and compositional transformations, which we investigated using Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) and Energy Dispersive Spectroscopy (EDS) analysis, and monitored its antibacterial characteristics using the Zone of Inhibition test (ZOI). SEM images confirmed the nanoparticles' morphology—agglomerated spherical, rod-like, and flake-like forms. EDS diagrams revealed the chemical composition, and ZOI results disclosed the antibacterial efficacy of doped versus undoped samples.
